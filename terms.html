Free and Premium

Whole Library swiping, up to 100 photos a day, is free. Duplicates, Best in Burst, Screenshots, Videos (including Shrink), Largest Files and unlimited daily swiping are Library Tamer Premium features. You can try every Premium feature free for 7 days; nothing is charged and nothing renews. After that, Premium is a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Subscriptions

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The monthly subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

Your photos are your responsibility

Library Tamer asks you to confirm before it changes your library, and iOS asks again before anything is deleted. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for about 30 days. Please keep a backup of photos that matter to you. To the extent the law allows, the developer isn't liable for photos or videos you choose to delete or shrink.
