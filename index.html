Tame your photo library one bite at a time. Swipe to keep or delete, find duplicates and bursts, clear out screenshots, and shrink big videos, a few minutes a day.

Common questions

I deleted something by mistake. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for about 30 days. Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted, and tap Recover.

Library Tamer only sees some of my photos. Go to Settings, then Apps, then Library Tamer, then Photos, and choose Full Access.

How do I restore my purchase? In the app, open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? Go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Library Tamer. Lifetime purchases never renew.
