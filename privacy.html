Privacy Policy

Short version: Library Tamer doesn't collect any of your data. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

Your photos

Library Tamer needs access to your photo library to show you photos and find duplicates, bursts and large files. All of that happens on your device. Your photos and videos are not uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone. Changes you make (deleting, favoriting, shrinking videos) are made by iOS on your device and sync through your own iCloud Photos if you use it.

What's stored on your device

The app keeps its settings and your progress (for example, which photos you've already kept) on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. I don't receive your name, email or payment details.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Library Tamer contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. If you send crash reports or feedback through Apple (for example, TestFlight), Apple shares that report with me under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Library Tamer doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
